UNDER THE HOOD

Built with
real-world care.

Waypoint is a flight discovery engineering project: real provider integration, isolated accounts, verified email alerts, bounded API usage, and an optional AI trip parser. Booking and payment happen outside this application.

Your data, your choice.

We store your email, password hash, sessions and saved alerts. Flight searches are cached for 10 minutes. Search details go to the flight provider. If you use AI input, that text goes to OpenAI. Email is delivered through the configured mail service. We do not collect payment cards or passport details. Delete your account to remove your saved data; backup copies expire under the operator's retention policy.

Clear boundaries.

Fares can change and are not reservations. Baggage and fare rules must be confirmed with the seller. The external link opens a route search, not a guaranteed offer or an affiliated checkout. Waypoint cannot issue, cancel or refund tickets.
